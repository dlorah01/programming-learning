# 📚 Programming Learning

A personal, systems-based programming mastery library. Every curriculum here is hand-built chapter by chapter — internals and execution model first, then the type/data model, then idioms and tooling, then performance and production concerns — aimed at deep, interview-ready fluency, not framework trivia.

Pick a track below to jump into its roadmap and chapters.

## [JavaScript](JavaScript/00-roadmap.md)
12 chapters, foundations through modern JS & interview synthesis, plus a full exercise answer key and an interactive mechanism-drill page. **Complete.**

## [Python](Python/00_roadmap.md)
20 modules, foundations through a cumulative interview sprint, covering the language, CPython internals, concurrency, memory, testing, and production engineering. **Complete.**

## [TypeScript](TypeScript/00-roadmap.md)
16 of 17 planned chapters, foundations through a capstone mock interview. **In progress** — the appendix (Master Exercise Reference, Ch. 17) is on the roadmap but not yet written.
